    கல்வெட்டு Inscription reader (experimental)

    What this can and cannot do. Everything runs in your browser; your photo is never uploaded. OpenCV cleans up the photo and finds letter-like shapes, then compares them with reference letters from three scripts to suggest which script, and so roughly which era, the inscription resembles. It cannot read ancient scripts word by word or date a stone exactly; for that, consult an epigraphist (for example the Archaeological Survey of India or the Tamil Nadu State Department of Archaeology). For modern Tamil lettering, "Read modern Tamil text" runs open-source OCR.
